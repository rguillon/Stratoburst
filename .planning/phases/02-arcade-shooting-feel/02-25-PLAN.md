<objective>
Make dying matter and respawning readable: a death costs two weapon levels and drops a recovery crystal of the current colour at the death spot, the ship explodes and disappears, and it comes back blinking with the hitbox dot always visible.

Purpose: PLYR-06 (death loses power, a recovery pickup drops nearby) and the visible half of PLYR-10 (respawn visibly blinking and invulnerable), with D-04, D-05 and D-15. This completes the death loop that plan 02-20 made lethal.
Output: WeaponLoadout.penalize, PickupPool.spawn_guaranteed, Game._on_death, the hull blink and ship hide, the PLAYER_DEATH and PLAYER_HIT recipes, and the death e2e level-loss case.
</objective>

This is the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal mapped to story slots; see 02-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

This plan's slice: dying costs me power but leaves a crystal to win some back, and I come back blinking.
