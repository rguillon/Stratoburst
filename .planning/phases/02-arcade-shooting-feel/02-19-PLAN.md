<objective>
Light the battlefield with explosion flashes from a fixed light pool, and give every aircraft a shadow on the ground that shows how high it flies.

Purpose: VISL-02 (dynamic lighting on top of bloom on emissive elements) and VISL-06 (air units cast readable shadows). Explosion lights reuse the fixed pool so shaders never recompile mid-game. Shadows are uncompensated blobs, so the offset itself is the altitude cue.
Output: light_pool.js, shadow_instances.js, tier `lights`, main.js wiring and the glow e2e assertions.
</objective>

This is the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal mapped to story slots; see 02-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

## Edge Probe: Classified Assumptions

- **VISL-02** (light edges):
  - More simultaneous blasts than enabled lights reuse the next light round-robin instead of adding lights. Resolved, as truth 2.
  - The low tier keeps the lights but at intensity 0. Resolved, as truth 2 (setEnabled(0) test).
  - The 3-light budget is affordable on phones (research assumption A5). Accepted, pending the plan 02-33 device gate, with `lights` per tier as the lever.
- **VISL-06** (shadow edges):
  - The shadow count equals the live air enemies plus the ship. Resolved, as truth 3.
  - A hidden ship casts no shadow. Resolved, as truth 4 (the shipVisible parameter, used by plan 02-25).
  - A single fixed altitude of 40 units serves every aircraft. Accepted. The assumption: one altitude reads correctly for a single-plane shmup.
