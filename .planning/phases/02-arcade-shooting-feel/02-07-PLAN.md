<objective>
Give the gallery's aircraft the same smooth, stylized, code-built look as the player's ship: jets, helicopters with spinning rotors, and distinctive carriers, each with glowing accents and tier-driven detail.

Purpose: VISL-01 (all models procedural in code), D-24 (smooth style) and D-25 (detail follows the tier). This plan replaces the primitive shapes from plan 02-04 for the air kinds and builds the body / glow / top instancing that the ground kinds reuse in plan 02-09. It is JavaScript only.
Output: jet.js, heli.js, carrier.js, the enemy model registry, upgraded enemy_instances.js and the main.js warm-up and tier wiring.
</objective>

**As a** player with the link, **I want to** fight a looping gallery of jets, helicopters, tanks and turrets with the full Raiden arsenal, bombs, death and respawn, **so that** shooting feels like a real 90s arcade shmup rendered in 3D. This is the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal mapped to story slots; see 02-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

This plan's slice: the jets, helicopters and carriers I fight look as polished as my own ship.
