<objective>
Put tanks and AA turrets on the ground visually: they are drawn on the terrain exactly where the bullets hit them, their explosions burst on the ground, and they leave charred wrecks that scroll away with the city.

Purpose: D-21 and STGE-04 (terrain-anchored ground targets and burning wrecks), ENMY-08 (wrecks on ground units) and VISL-03 (LARGE explosions placed where heavies die). It also runs the automated check of research assumption A4: the terrain sits 40 units below the gameplay plane, so ground visuals need a ray-compensating transform to line up with the hitboxes on the plane. Plan 02-14 adds scorch marks and the smouldering trickle.
Output: ground_layer.js, the rig refit hook, ground-kind parenting in enemy_instances, ground-aware explosion positioning, wreck_ring.js, main.js wiring and the ground e2e spec.
</objective>

## Edge Probe: Classified Assumptions

  - Hit alignment holds at portrait, 4:3 and 16:9. Resolved, as truth 2 (NDC test, research assumption A4).
  - Wrecks scroll at the ground ratio and leave at the bottom. Resolved, as truth 4.
  - The oldest of 12 wrecks is overwritten when full. Resolved, as truth 4.
  - "Persistent" means the wreck lasts until it scrolls off the bottom, not for the whole session. Accepted. The assumption: in a vertically scrolling shmup, ground debris that has left the screen is never seen again.

  - A ground kill at the very bottom edge still records a wreck that leaves at once. Accepted. The assumption: a wreck nobody sees costs one ring slot for a few frames.
