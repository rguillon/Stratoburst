<objective>
Make big events hit hard: the bomb and the player's death freeze the action for a few frames and shake the screen, while ordinary hits and kills stay smooth.

Purpose: VISL-04 (shake and short hitstop on big events) with D-26 and D-27, and ROADMAP criterion 5 ("ordinary hits do not"). Boss parts and boss deaths reuse the same mechanism in Phase 3. The freeze is simulation-side and deterministic; the shake is a render-only camera offset.
Output: Hitstop in Python with the `_step` gate and the HITSTOP header slot; shake.js and the frozen effect clock in JS; e2e assertions on the bomb and death specs.
</objective>
