<objective>
Make the weapon state testable and visible: allow-listed `?weapon` and `?level` flags validated on both sides of the boundary, a small status readout of the weapon and level, and bolts that carry their flight angle.

Purpose: PLYR-05 and PLYR-04 become observable on the live link. This is the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal mapped to story slots; see 02-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

This plan's slice: I can always see which weapon and level I have, and a tester can jump straight to any of them with a link.
