<objective>
Replace the Phase 1 drones with the shooting gallery's simulation core: an enemy pool with kind tables, fed by a looping spawn script, hit through the grid broadphase and exploding at its own blast class.

Purpose: D-17 and D-20 (the gallery replaces the drones and is the default game) and ENMY-08 (flash on hit, explode on destruction). This is the foundation slice; plan 02-04 renders the enemies per kind and renames the wire names. The EnemyPool and its kind tables are the base for every later system (weapons, ground units, enemy fire, bomb, homing). The spawner lives in `stages/`, beside the terrain scroll, as the stand-in for the Phase 3 stage timeline. It is a plain script loop that does not block that design.
Output: EnemyKind, EnemyPool, GallerySpawner and resolve_shots_vs_enemies, the deletion of DroneSwarm, and the Game wiring, all with tests.
</objective>

This is the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal mapped to story slots; see 02-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

This plan's slice (foundation): the targets I shoot come from a scripted gallery of typed enemies instead of random drones.
