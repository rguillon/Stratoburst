<objective>
Make enemy bullets readable against fire and bloom: pink orbs with a white core and a dark rim, drawn in a dedicated pass after bloom on every tier, with pink reserved by a tested palette. Player bolts become stretched HDR tracers.

Purpose: VISL-05 (enemy bullets readable, D-23) and the bullet half of VISL-02 (bullets glow under bloom). This lands before enemies start shooting (plan 02-18, same wave, bullets harmless until plan 02-20), so the bullets that can kill the player are readable from their first lethal frame. Until then the `?stress` storm bullets are the enemy bullets on screen.
Output: palette.js with the reserved-pink test, the orb and bolt bullet styles, the post-bloom bullet pass, recipe colours from the palette, and the glow e2e spec.
</objective>
