<objective>
Ship the stress mode early, because it is the phase's hardest constraint (the go/no-go). `?stress=N` puts N Python-driven bullets on screen under real bloom. The 3 ms desktop Python budget becomes a failing check, and the hot loop is proven allocation-stable.

Purpose: PLAT-04 (60 fps with 400+ bullets, effects on) and D-15 `?stress=N`. RESEARCH says the unmeasured risk is fill rate and bloom, so bloom must exist and be measured from here on.
Output: BulletPool, BulletStorm and circles_touch in Python; the instanced bullet renderer and the bloom pipeline in JS; the Pyodide-on-Node perf harness; the stress e2e spec.
</objective>
