<objective>
Turn the stress mode into a measurement instrument. Automatic quality tiers step down safely: they never thrash on a 30 Hz-capped phone and never touch gameplay. An on-device overlay and a copyable report produce the numbers the go/no-go gate (plan 01-10) decides on.

Purpose: PLAT-05 (≥30 fps steady on phones via automatic quality tiers) and the PLAT-04 measurement side; D-15 `?debug` and `?tier=`. RESEARCH: "the phase's real deliverable is the measurement tooling … the go/no-go decision is made from that report, not from the code."
Output: tiers.js, quality_controller.js, scaled_bloom_pass.js, frame_stats.js, overlay.js, report.js, tier-aware rig and pipeline, vitest coverage of the pure logic, and the debug e2e spec.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** friend with just the link, **I want to** load the game on my desktop or phone and fly a ship that shoots down targets over scrolling 3D terrain, **so that** I can play a Raiden-style shmup in the browser with no install or server. This is the ROADMAP Phase 1 goal mapped to story slots; see 01-01-PLAN.md for the sourcing note.

This plan's slice: on a slow phone the game lowers its own visual cost to stay smooth, and a tester can copy an exact performance report.
