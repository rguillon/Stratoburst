## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal (verbatim): "Player can play a complete stage 1, with scripted air and ground waves over a destructible burning city and a multi-phase boss at the end, plus scoring, lives and a HUD. This proves the content pipeline before more stages are added."

**As a** player with the link, **I want to** play a complete stage 1 (scripted air and ground waves over a destructible burning neon city, a mid-boss and a multi-phase bomber boss, with scoring, medals, lives and a HUD), **so that** it plays like a real 90s arcade stage from start to boss and the content pipeline is proven before stages 2-3 are built. (Story sourcing: see 03-01-PLAN.md.)

This plan's slice: the whole stage, judged on real devices.

Why the device checkpoint is `gate="blocking-human"` even though `workflow.human_verify_mode` is end-of-phase: this plan is the end-of-phase review, the verdict cannot be computed before the measurements exist, and auto-approving it would fabricate a pass (the plan 02-33 reasoning).
